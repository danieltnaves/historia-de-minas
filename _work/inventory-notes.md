## Transcription conventions
- `<!-- p. N -->` at the start of each book page. No running headers / page numbers.
- Chapter start: `<h2 id="cNN">Title</h2>` (NN = 01..24, order of Indice).
- Paragraph cut by page end: end it with `</p><!--cont-->`; next page's first paragraph is `<p class="c">`. Assembler merges. A word split across the page break: write the whole word at the end of the FIRST page and omit the remainder on the next.
- Keep the original 1938 spelling exactly (incl. "á" for "à", "elle", "historia", "Bandeirantes", etc.). Em dash "—", quotes “ ”.
- Italic -> <em>, bold -> <strong>. Printed ALL-CAPS / SMALL-CAPS kept as printed.
- Footnote ref: `<sup><a href="#fn-pN-K">(K)</a></sup>`; footnote: `<p class="fn" id="fn-pN-K"><sup>(K)</sup> text</p>` at the end of that page.
- Figure: `<figure class="plate"><img src="images/fig-pNNN.jpg" alt="..."></figure>` placed where it prints.
- Unreadable letters: `[?]`. Schools stamp "Grupo Escolar de Christina - Sul de Minas" ignored. "H. T. M. — N" signature marks ignored.
- Verse: `<p class="verse">line<br>line</p>`.
- ASSEMBLER JOIN RULE: for `</p><!--cont-->` + (anything: page comments, figures, footnotes) + `<p class="c">`: delete the two tags, join the text with a single space, and move the "anything" to right after the merged paragraph. Chains (p16->p17->p18) repeat.
- Prefacio `<section class="front">` opens in text/p003.html and closes at the end of text/p004.html.
